5. Strategic QKD vs. PQC Capital Allocation

NIST FIPS 203 ML-KEM-768 is the mandatory universal software standard for all web, VPN, and database endpoints. Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) is recommended strictly as a physical-layer defense-in-depth supplement for high-value fixed metro optical links (e.g. PACS-01) where fiber distance remains under the 203 km unrepeatered limit.
